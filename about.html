Our story, in a nutshell

It started with a recipe, and the people we love.

CAKE BLOOM began in a home kitchen with handwritten recipes, flour-dusted aprons, and the kind of Sunday afternoons that always ended around the table. We wanted to bottle that feeling: the warmth of being thought of, the joy of an unexpected treat, the little pause before everyone says, “one more slice?”

So we kept baking. Today our small team still makes each cake in thoughtful batches, with ingredients we believe in and the same generous spirit we started with. We’re here for birthdays, big beginnings, quiet Tuesdays, and all the in-between moments worth celebrating.
